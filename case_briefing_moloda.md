### 1. DETAINEE DETAILS
* **Name:** Valentyna Moloda (Валентина Молода)
* **Age:** 49
* **Profession:** Physician / director of private medical clinic (Kyiv)
* **Current Status:** Pretrial detention in Kyiv SIZO for 60 days without bail (Order of Solomianskyi District Court of Kyiv, 4 November 2026; term expires 2 January 2027)
* **Charges:**
  * Article 114-1, Part 1, Criminal Code of Ukraine (Obstruction of lawful activities of the Armed Forces of Ukraine and other military formations during a special period; 5 to 8 years imprisonment)
  * Article 114-2, Part 2, Criminal Code of Ukraine (Unauthorized dissemination of information on the movement or location of the AFU or military formations under martial law; 5 to 8 years imprisonment)

---

### 2. FACTUAL SUMMARY
* **Context:** In early November 2026, reports surfaced regarding an unacknowledged holding facility located in Petropavlivska Borshchahivka (Kyiv region) under National Guard control, housing mobilized persons and soldiers subject to unauthorized absence (SZCh) proceedings.
* **Incident (2–3 November 2026):** Valentyna Moloda arrived at the premises in medical attire utilizing an ambulance-marked vehicle to access an individual allegedly confined inside who reportedly required medical assistance.
* **Public Broadcast:** Upon denial of entry, Moloda initiated a live stream on TikTok alleging unlawful detention and lack of medical attention inside the basement, prompting relatives and local residents to assemble outside.
* **Intervention and Detention:** State security forces (SBU, National Police) dispersed the crowd, detained Moloda, and seized communications equipment.

**Prosecution Submissions:**
The SBU and Kyiv Regional Prosecutor's Office allege an organized scheme to extract an AWOL serviceman under the guise of medical transport for financial consideration (approx. 107,000 UAH), arguing that the live broadcast was intended to provoke civil unrest and obstruct military personnel.

**Defense Submissions:**
Moloda and counsel submit that her actions were solely medical and humanitarian, responding to an urgent request from an acquaintance regarding a detainee in critical physical condition. The defense denies organizing unrest or unlawfully disseminating operational military information.

---

### 3. LEGAL CONCERNS UNDER INTERNATIONAL LAW
1. **Unacknowledged Detention Facilities (Article 9 ICCPR, Article 5 ECHR):**
   The incident exposes the operation of informal detention facilities where individuals are held without formal arrest records, court warrants, or prompt access to statutory defense counsel.
2. **Disproportionate Criminalization of Monitoring (Articles 19 ICCPR, Article 10 ECHR):**
   The application of national security provisions (Articles 114-1 and 114-2) against individuals documenting or publicizing alleged extrajudicial custody sets a restrictive precedent that suppresses civilian oversight and whistleblower reporting.
3. **Mandatory Custody Without Bail (Article 9(3) ICCPR, Article 5(3) ECHR):**
   Pursuant to Article 176(5) of the Criminal Procedure Code of Ukraine, detention without bail was imposed without individualized assessment of flight or obstruction risk for a non-violent offense.
4. **Violation of Presumption of Innocence (Article 14(2) ICCPR, Article 6(2) ECHR):**
   Statements by official security agencies and state-aligned media have attributed guilt prior to judicial adjudication, characterizing the detainee as an operational asset conducting hostile foreign information-psychological operations ("IPSO").

---

### 4. REQUESTED ACTIONS
1. Issue urgent communications to the Government of Ukraine regarding the legal basis of custody for persons held at the Petropavlivska Borshchahivka facility.
2. Request confirmation from judicial authorities that appellate review will assess alternative non-custodial measures in compliance with international standards.
3. Request diplomatic and NGO observation missions to attend open appellate hearings in Kyiv.
4. Ensure access to independent medical evaluation and uninhibited confidential consultation with private defense counsel.
